High-Elevation Sanctuaries

The Cabins

Crafted from raw native timber, local river rock, and massive glass panes to bring the quiet grandeur of the deep forest inside.

Designed to slow you down.

Our three signature cabin layouts stand spaced apart on the mountain ridge, ensuring complete isolation and uncompromised views of the pine canopy.

The Premier Sanctuary

The Ridge House

Positioned on the highest granite outcropping of the property, the Ridge House features soaring double-height ceilings, a massive floor-to-ceiling stone hearth, and a private cedar deck that hangs directly over the canyon edge.

  • Sleeps: Up to 4 Guests
  • Beds: 1 King, 1 Queen Loft
  • Baths: 2 Full Baths
  • Size: 1,400 sq ft
Grounded Isolation

Tamarack Cabin

Nestled deep in a dense stand of ancient tamarack pines, this cabin focuses inward on pure comfort. It features raw timber walls, deep leather furnishings, a stone-walled rainfall shower, and a wood-fired hot tub directly off the master patio.

  • Sleeps: 2 Guests (Adults Only)
  • Beds: 1 California King
  • Baths: 1 Bath + Outdoor Tub
  • Size: 950 sq ft
Minimalist Refuge

The Outpost Suite

Designed for the quiet writer or solo traveler, the Outpost is our most intimate structure. A masterfully crafted studio with a corner woodstove, integrated desk workspace overlooking the valley, and custom built-in pine cabinetry.

  • Sleeps: 1 to 2 Guests
  • Beds: 1 Built-in Queen
  • Baths: 1 Bath with Copper Tub
  • Size: 600 sq ft
Honest Craftsmanship

The Cabin Ground Facts

Every shelter is tailored with a foundational standard of luxury and remote comfort.

Structural Elements

The Timber
Hand-scribed native Douglas Fir logs and reclaimed lodgepole pine planks.
Heating
Radiant in-floor slate tile heating supplemented by raw wood-burning stoves.
Glazing
Triple-pane architectural glass framed in steel, optimizing forest vistas and insulation.
Fireplaces
Hand-laid river stone chimneys with heavy iron woodstoves, stocked daily with dry pine cordwood.

Inclusions & Grounds

Soaking
Private outdoor wood-fired cedar hot tubs at the Ridge House and Tamarack Cabin.
Connectivity
High-speed Starlink fiber connection with integrated timber writing desks in every suite.
Kitchens
Cast iron cooktops, pour-over coffee bar featuring local roasts, and under-counter refrigeration.
Pet Policy
Well-behaved dogs are welcome with reservation. Direct trailhead access right outside your door.
