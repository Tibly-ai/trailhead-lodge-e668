Grounded & Honest

Slow mountain living, hand-carved into the wild ridges of Silverton.

At The Trailhead Lodge, we believe the best spaces ask very little of you, yet offer absolute quiet. Each cabin has been restored using raw timbers sourced from fallen local pine, built to withstand the crisp mountain air and host generations of weary searchers.

Stoke the hearth fire, brew a slow coffee with natural spring water, and watch the low light filter through the dense canopy of ancient hemlocks.

"We wanted to build something that felt like it had grown directly out of the mountain floor."
— The Trailhead Builders

Specs & Sanctuary

An honest space to reset.

We state our spaces plainly: raw elements, quiet corners, and exactly what you need to disconnect from modern noise.

Designed for Stillness

Every material is organic, locally sourced, and untreated by harsh synthetic finishes. We believe the pine oils inside the timber carry the true scent of healing.

The Property Blueprint

Capacity Each cabin sleeps up to 4 guests comfortably. No crowds, no noise.
Sleeping Quarters 1 King-size master bed in raw pine frame, 1 Queen-size sleeping loft, draped in heavy natural wool textiles.
Bathrooms 1 full bath featuring raw stone rain showers and spring-fed heated water systems.
Forest Cedar Hot Tub Each cabin features a private wood-fired cedar-plank hot tub preheated daily by request.
Wilderness Views Floor-to-ceiling vistas looking over the pine valley and the steep granite slopes of the San Juan peak line.
Connectivity High-speed satellite connection available. Silent workstations built from solid rough-sawn pine.
Pet Policy We welcome dogs on leash. Hand-carved pine dog runs exist throughout the lodge grounds.
