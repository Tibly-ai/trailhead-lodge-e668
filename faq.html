Mountain Preparation

Essential Stay Information & House Guidelines

Everything you need to know to transition smoothly from the busy world into our quiet high-alpine forest. Read through our core guidelines, seasonal advice, and practical answers.

The Lodge Rules

We preserve a quiet sanctuary for both travelers and the surrounding forest wildlife. Thank you for respecting these boundaries during your mountain escape.

  • ✦ Check-in & Check-out: Arrival begins at 4:00 PM. We ask that you depart by 11:00 AM so we can thoroughly prep the wood interiors for the next arrivals.
  • ✦ Quiet Hours: Under the canopy, sound travels easily. We observe deep silence hours from 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM.
  • ✦ Fire Safety: Due to our high-elevation pine forest location, outdoor fires are permitted strictly in the communal fire pit during low-risk seasons. Indoor timber stoves require careful handling.
  • ✦ Wildlife & Food Storage: This is active bear and elk country. Absolutely no food or scented supplies may be left in outdoor areas or vehicles.

High-Altitude Note

Our cabins rest at 9,300 feet above sea level. We recommend drinking double your normal water intake and taking things slow during your first twenty-four hours in the San Juans.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there cellular reception or wireless internet at the cabins?

We offer complimentary high-speed fiber-optic wireless network access throughout all individual cabins and the central lodge structure. Cell reception can vary by carrier, but Wi-Fi calling is fully supported to keep you connected if you choose.

What kind of vehicle is required to reach the property?

During late spring, summer, and early autumn, standard front-wheel-drive vehicles reach our primary parking lot with ease. From November through April, a four-wheel-drive or all-wheel-drive vehicle with winter tires is mandatory due to snow accumulation.

Do you allow dogs or other pets?

We welcome well-behaved dogs in our designated pet-friendly cabins with a small cleaning fee per stay. Please reach out during your reservation request to secure a pet-friendly option, as they are limited. Dogs must remain leashed while walking on the property trails.

Are the kitchens fully equipped?

Each cabin kitchen features premium gas stoves, refrigerators, kettles, French presses, cookware, tableware, and basic spices. We supply locally roasted coffee grounds from Silverton for your mornings.

What is your cancellation policy?

Reservations cancelled thirty days or more prior to arrival receive a full refund minus a standard processing fee. Cancellations within thirty days of your stay are non-refundable unless we can re-book the cabin dates.
