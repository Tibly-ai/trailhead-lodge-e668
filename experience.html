High-Elevation Trails & Alpine Lakes

The Trailhead Lodge rests directly where the pine forest meets the high country. Step outside your timber porch to access winding dirt singletracks, glacial valleys, and forgotten mountain ruins.

Alpine Lake Hikes

Three glacier lakes rest within a two-hour moderate walk from the front lodge. We provide handcrafted wool-bound maps and packed field lunches.

Wade into cold mountain streams with local guides who know every hidden pool and pocket where trout rise at dawn.
